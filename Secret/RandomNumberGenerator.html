❓ FAQ

What does this generator do?

It generates random numbers inside the range you choose.

What is Guess Mode?

Guess Mode lets you enter a number before generating. The generator tells you how close your guess was.

What is Impossible Mode?

Impossible Mode chooses one secret number and keeps it hidden. Every attempt uses the same number until you guess it correctly.

What does the streak mean?

Your streak increases whenever you guess correctly. An incorrect guess resets your current streak.

What is the best streak?

Your best streak is the highest number of consecutive correct guesses you've achieved during the current session.

What is the closest guess?

This is the guess that has come closest to the generated number.

What is average distance?

This is the average difference between your guesses and the numbers they were trying to predict.

What does Generate Multiple do?

It generates several random numbers at once and displays them in the result area.

Does this save my statistics?

No. This app does not use localStorage. Refreshing the page resets everything.

Can I use decimal numbers?

Yes. Enable Decimal mode and choose how many decimal places you want.
