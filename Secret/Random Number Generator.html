Frequently Asked Questions

How does this work?

The generator creates a random number between the minimum and maximum values you provide.

What is Guess Mode?

Guess Mode lets you enter a number before generating. The generator then tells you how far away your guess was.

What is Impossible Mode?

Impossible Mode hides the generated number. You must keep guessing until you find it.

Every guess tells you whether you were too high or too low, as well as how far away you were.

What does "distance" mean?

Distance is the absolute difference between your guess and the generated number.

Can I generate decimals?

Yes. Change Number Type to Decimals and choose how many decimal places you want.

Does the website save my statistics?

No. Statistics only exist while this page is open. Refreshing the page resets everything.

Can I change the range?

Yes. You can enter any minimum and maximum values you want.
